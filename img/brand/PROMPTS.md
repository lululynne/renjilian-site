## 主方向整理

Use case: logo-brand. Edit an existing approved direction into a production site mark.
Input image 1 is the sole edit target and identity reference: the supplied smaller-pink-heart finger-and-paw illustration. Do not combine the five draft alternatives.
Create one square 1:1 PNG with a genuinely transparent alpha background for the Chinese publication Second Person. No text, letters, wordmark, watermark, wood, hanging ropes, scene background or square backing panel.
Keep the identity and pose: a warm peach human hand enters from upper left, with its index finger extended diagonally inward; a cream-white cat paw enters from upper right, its pink pads facing the viewer. Their tips gently touch at the center. Behind them sits one moderately sized bright pink heart, the bottom-most layer. The cat paw has one heart-shaped central pad and four small toe pads. Preserve the gentle warm sticker illustration style, warm dark-brown contours and restrained ivory sticker outline.
Recompose the complete existing motif for a square icon. Keep every limb end and the entire heart inside the canvas. Place all visible content inside the central 80 percent circular safe area, with balanced transparent breathing room on all sides. The fingertip-paw contact must be the focal point. Use broad clean silhouettes and only a single small golden four-point sparkle at their contact; remove the draft's many tiny sparkles and diffuse rays so it remains readable at 32 pixels. Keep the heart smaller than the whole hand-and-paw silhouette, as in this reference. Do not add floating hearts, characters, extra hands or extra digits. Use clean soft cel shading rather than photographic texture or 3D rendering.
The result is a final cutout asset, not a presentation board or a logo mockup. Real transparency is required, including outside the sticker outline.
